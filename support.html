How do I play?

Tap to hop forward. Swipe left, right, or down to move sideways or back. Tap Long jump to leap two rows when you have a charge. Keep moving: the island crumbles behind you.

Can I make the game easier?

Tap the card at the top of the home screen to open Your run, then select Easy. It has slower traffic, a slower crumble, and three hearts per run. Medium and Hard are available in the same menu.

How do I unlock other eras?

Open Your run from the top card on the home screen. Today is available from the start. Jurassic, Cretaceous, Ice Age, and Ancient Egypt each unlock for 400 gems earned by playing. Hopfall is free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Can I reduce movement and adjust sound?

Open Settings using the gear icon. Turn off Camera moves to reduce camera movement, shake, and flashes. You can also change vibration, move the Long jump button to the right, choose a sound pack, and adjust Music and Effects volume separately.

Does Hopfall need an internet connection?

Gameplay works offline. The daily run is generated using the date on your device; it does not need to download a level. Opening these web pages, sending email, or sharing through another app may require a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved locally on your device. Hopfall has no account-based cloud-save service, so the developer does not have a server copy to restore. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups may contain app data, depending on your backup settings. Avoid deleting and reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step.

How do I share a score?

Use Share after a run, then choose a destination in the system share sheet. The score picture is created from the game. Available destinations depend on the apps and services on your device.

Something still isn’t working

Try closing and reopening Hopfall, check for an available app update, and email support if the problem continues. For sound problems, check the Music and Effects sliders in Settings and your device’s volume and audio output.
