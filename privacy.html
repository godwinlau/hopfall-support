Hopfall for iPhone

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Hopfall is an offline game by Godwin Laureto. This policy explains the game’s local data, optional sharing, support email, and these web pages.

During gameplay

Hopfall does not require an account. The game has no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not send your saved progress or gameplay activity to the developer.

Data saved on your device

The game stores your scores, gems, unlocked characters and eras, challenge and mission progress, daily-run results and streak, run statistics, and settings locally. It also stores movement and timing data from your best runs so you can race a “ghost” of a previous run. This is game-movement data, not a camera or microphone recording.

This data is used to remember your progress and run the game’s features. Hopfall does not provide a developer-operated cloud-save or account service. Your device’s backup or transfer features may include app data; see Apple’s information about iCloud backups.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. Copies may remain in device backups, or in score images you have saved or shared. Backup copies are managed through your device and backup provider.

When you choose to share a score

Tapping Share creates a picture of your game and score, together with a short score message. The app writes the image to its local cache and opens the system share sheet. The app or recipient you choose receives the content you share, under that service’s own privacy practices. Hopfall does not automatically upload the image to the developer.

When you email support

If you email winlaureto@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, any sender name, and the message and attachments you choose to send, so your request can be answered and problems investigated. These messages are handled through Gmail; Google’s Privacy Policy describes Google’s handling of information.

Only include information relevant to your question. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. You can use the same email address for questions about your support correspondence or to request its deletion.

Visiting these web pages

These support and privacy pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or remotely loaded fonts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children and privacy

The game has no accounts, in-game chat, or forms requesting personal information. A parent or guardian can contact the developer with a support request or privacy question.

Questions and updates

Contact Godwin Laureto at winlaureto@gmail.com with privacy questions. Any updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date.
